Design language · v2 · glass + colour wheel

Wavelength, in full colour

Version 2 of the mockups. Panels are frosted glass over soft colour light. One spectrum runs round the colour wheel from green to violet to red, and every bar, icon and meter takes its colour from that wheel. Records become CDs and cassettes, and there are two new pages: Song DNA and Discover.

Foundations

The spectrum is the only accent. Its order means something. Navigation icons take the colour at their place down the sidebar, months take theirs across the year, and meters fill from green to red.

Spectrum · green → teal → blue → violet → magenta → red

Typeface · Figtree

Top tracksMost of your listening happens on weekday evenings.Spotify's own typeface (Spotify Mix) isn't free to use, so Figtree, a close geometric match, stands in.

Glass recipe

White fill at 3–10%, a 22px background blur with saturation pushed to 160%, a 1px border at 14% white and a light top edge.Text stays on solid-enough glass to keep contrast above 4.5:1.

Works
Popularity, followers

Removed from track, album and artist objects for development-mode apps (Feb–Mar 2026). v1's popularity meters can't be built.

Removed
Song DNA: dance, energy, mood, tempo, key

Spotify closed Audio Features to new apps on 27 Nov 2024. ReccoBeats returns the same fields from a Spotify track ID with no key, and allows calls from 127.0.0.1. Tested live.

Third-party
Recommended songs

Spotify's endpoint is closed. ReccoBeats /track/recommendation takes Spotify IDs as seeds. Tested live; results vary in quality.

Third-party
Recommended artists and albums

Related Artists is closed. Take the artists and albums of the recommended songs, then fetch album art one album at a time (bulk fetches were removed).

Workaround
Save a generated playlist

Spotify POST /me/playlists, then POST /playlists/{id}/items. Needs the playlist-modify-private scope.

Works
Who can use it

Up to 5 users you add by hand, and the app owner needs Spotify Premium.
