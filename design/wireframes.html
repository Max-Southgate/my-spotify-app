Wavelength: your listening, drawn like sound

A new look for the listening-stats app. Today every screen is the same light card on a green glow. Here each kind of data gets its own instrument from a studio: time becomes a waveform, scores become level meters, artists become records, genres become an EQ and songs become a tracklist.

Navigation

  • Desktop gets a sidebar, phones get a bottom bar. Both have five items, the most a bottom bar should hold.
  • Recently played joins Overview, which takes the nav from six tabs to five.
  • The time range is one switch at the top of each page, with the same three choices everywhere.

Colour has a job

  • Amber means "selected" or "this is data". It is used for nothing else.
  • Green, amber and red appear only inside meters, so they never compete with buttons.
  • Body text is 17:1 on ink, and grey text is above 7:1 on panels.

Building it in this codebase

  • The colour and font tokens at the top of this page drop straight into App.css as CSS variables. The fonts go in public/index.html.
  • Waveforms, meters, EQ bars and vinyl are plain CSS, so there's no new library. Chart.js can stay for the month chart, or be replaced by CSS bars.
  • The heatmap needs one new loop over endTime, which already includes the hour.
