11 · Next

What each feature needs

Sizes are rough: S is an evening or two, M is a weekend, L is longer. ReccoBeats is a free third-party API. It was tested in November 2026 with Spotify track IDs and accepts calls from this app's address.

FeatureWhat it needsSizeWatch out for
Drop-in data export

A file picker and the same parsing the app does today. No new service.

S

Large files take a few seconds to read.

Full history since you joined

Request "Extended streaming history" in your Spotify privacy settings. Same drop-in page.

S–M

Spotify can take up to 30 days to send it.

Song DNA

ReccoBeats audio features, looked up by Spotify track ID, plus Chart.js, which the app already uses.

M

Some tracks may have no data. It depends on a third party staying up.

Recommended songs, artists, albums

ReccoBeats recommendations seeded with your top tracks. Album art comes from Spotify, one album at a time.

M

Quality varies by seed. Spotify's own recommendations are closed to this app.

Playlist generator

The two above, plus Spotify's create-playlist and add-items endpoints and the playlist-modify-private permission.

M–L

Users must log in again to grant the new permission.

All artists, tracks and numbers on this page are made-up sample data.
